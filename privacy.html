SecMobApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information Access

With the user's explicit authorization, SecMobApp may access Gmail messages using the Gmail read-only permission. The application does not ask users to provide their Google password.

Purpose of Use

Email content is accessed to analyze potential phishing, suspicious links, and other security indicators.

Storage and Sharing

Security analysis and history are intended to be stored locally on the user's device. Google OAuth tokens are used to authorize API access. The application does not sell personal information.

User Control

Users can choose whether to connect Gmail and can disconnect their account through the application's available controls.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email listed on the SecMobApp Google authorization page.
